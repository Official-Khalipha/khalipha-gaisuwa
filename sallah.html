Yadda Ake Sallah - Haske Na Musulunci

1. Alwala

Fara da Bismillah, wanke hannu sau 3, bakin sau 3, hanci, fuska, hannu zuwa gwiwar hannu, shafar kai, kunne, da kafa.

2. Niyya da Kabbarar Harama

Yi niyya a zuciyarka, sannan kace Allahu Akbar.

3. Karatun Fatiha da Surah

Karanta Fatiha sannan wata karamar sura kamar Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad.

4. Ruku'u da Sujuda

Yi Ruku'u kace Subhana Rabbiyal Azeem sau 3. Tashi, sannan Sujuda kace Subhana Rabbiyal A'ala sau 3.
